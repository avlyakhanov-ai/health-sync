health-sync

Personal, single-user tool

health-sync is a small script that its author runs on his own computer to export his own sleep and health data from the Google Health API (formerly the Fitbit Web API) into local CSV files for personal analysis of sleep quality.

It requests read-only access to sleep, health metrics (heart rate, heart rate variability, oxygen saturation, breathing rate, skin temperature) and activity (steps) data. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not write, share or sell any data.
