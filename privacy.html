Privacy policy

health-sync · effective 20 November 2026

Who uses the app

health-sync is a personal tool used by a single person, its author, to access his own data. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the user's consent through Google OAuth, the app reads, with read-only scopes, the user's own data from the Google Health API: sleep sessions and stages, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, oxygen saturation, breathing rate, skin temperature and daily steps. It does not request write access.

How the data is used and stored

The data is downloaded to the user's own computer and stored there as local files for personal analysis of sleep and health. OAuth tokens are stored locally with access restricted to the user's account. The app has no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising, credit decisions or training of generalized AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

health-sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Deleting the local files removes all data the app has downloaded.

Contact

Questions about this policy: GitHub issues.
